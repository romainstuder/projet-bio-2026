About

I am a bioinformatics data scientist specializing in the study of proteins and nucleotides, with a focus on their impact on human health and drug discovery. With over 15 years of experience spanning academic research and industry, I bring deep expertise in integrating diverse datasets—such as sequences, 3D structures, and gene ontology—into automated workflows that drive biomedical research and therapeutic innovation.

My career includes 8 years at BenevolentAI (progressing to Lead Bioinformatics Data Scientist), where I worked on drug-target-disease relationships, integrated large-scale datasets in Databricks, and supported collaborations with pharmaceutical partners including Novartis and AstraZeneca. Prior to that, I completed a postdoctoral fellowship at EMBL-EBI and research positions at UCL, studying protein evolution and natural selection.

I hold a PhD from the University of Lausanne, where my thesis focused on the impact of natural selection on large-scale protein datasets. Currently, I work on computational protein design—de novo generation with diffusion models and inverse folding (RFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN), and structure-based antibody affinity engineering—building end-to-end pipelines that combine AlphaFold, Boltz-2, and ESM2 for therapeutic applications. I have authored 26 peer-reviewed publications and received multiple grants and awards including the SNSF fellowship.

Services

🧬

Structural Bioinformatics

Expert analysis of protein 3D structures, pocket identification, homology modeling, and stability analysis. Leveraging tools like AlphaFold, PyMOL, Modeller, and FoldX to solve structural biology challenges in drug discovery.

🤖

AI-Driven Protein Design

Cutting-edge generative protein design combining structure prediction (AlphaFold, Boltz-2), protein language model embeddings (ESM2), diffusion-based generation (RFdiffusion), and inverse folding (ProteinMPNN). Building end-to-end pipelines for therapeutic protein engineering, antibody design, and novel enzyme discovery.

📊

Data Integration & Pipeline Development

Building robust bioinformatics workflows integrating diverse datasets (sequences, structures, genomics, clinical data). Production-grade Python/R packages, Databricks integration, and cloud deployment (AWS, Kubernetes).

🎯

Drug Discovery Support

Target identification, drug-target-disease relationship modeling, knowledge graph design, and interrogation of large perturbation datasets (LINCS). Supporting pharma/biotech in early-stage discovery and target validation.

🧪

Sequence Analysis & Evolution

Comprehensive sequence analysis including BLAST, HMMER searches, multiple sequence alignment, phylogenetics, and molecular evolution studies. RNAseq analysis, differential expression, and gene set enrichment.

📈

Machine Learning & Statistical Modeling

Applying supervised and unsupervised ML methods (Random Forest, XGBoost, PCA, clustering) to biological data. Statistical analysis, longitudinal data modeling, and clinical data stratification for endotype identification.

🔬

Antibody Affinity Engineering

Structure-based antibody–antigen affinity maturation using FoldX: interface identification, CDR-aware PSSM scanning across all substitutions, and ΔΔG combinatorial multi-mutant design. Delivering ranked candidate sequences for experimental validation, parallelised for HPC.

⚙️

LLM-Assisted Automation

Designing LLM-assisted development patterns (Claude Code) to automate multistep bioinformatics analyses—accelerating pipeline development, reducing manual overhead, and establishing reusable building blocks for therapeutic-design workflows.
