About Studer Bio

Senior computational-biology expertise, available to your team.

Studer Bio brings over 15 years of bioinformatics and computational-biology experience—across both academic research and industry—to drug-target-disease programmes in pharma, biotech, and academia. The focus is turning heterogeneous biological data (sequences, 3D structures, omics, clinical, and chemical) into automated, production-grade workflows that move research forward.

That experience includes 8 years at BenevolentAI—progressing to Lead Bioinformatics Data Scientist—with hands-on delivery across drug-target-disease modelling, large-scale data integration, and collaborations with pharmaceutical partners such as Novartis and AstraZeneca. It is built on a research foundation from EMBL-EBI, UCL, and a PhD from the University of Lausanne.

Current work centres on computational protein design—de novo generation with diffusion models and inverse folding, and structure-based antibody affinity engineering. The practice is led by Romain Studer, PhD, author of 26 peer-reviewed publications and recipient of the SNSF fellowship.
