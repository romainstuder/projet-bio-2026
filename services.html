Services

End-to-end computational biology—from exploratory analysis to production-grade pipelines.

Studer Bio offers focused, senior-level support across the following areas. Engagements range from one-off analyses to long-term pipeline development.

🧬

Structural Bioinformatics

Expert analysis of protein 3D structures, pocket identification, homology modelling, and stability-effect prediction. Leveraging tools like AlphaFold and PyMOL to solve structural biology challenges in drug discovery.

🤖

AI-Driven Protein Design

Cutting-edge generative protein design combining structure prediction (AlphaFold, Boltz-2), protein language model embeddings (ESM2), diffusion-based generation (RFdiffusion), and inverse folding (ProteinMPNN). Building end-to-end pipelines for therapeutic protein engineering, antibody design, and novel enzyme discovery.

📊

Data Integration & Pipeline Development

Building robust bioinformatics workflows integrating diverse datasets (sequences, structures, genomics, clinical data). Production-grade Python/R packages, Databricks integration, and cloud deployment (AWS, Kubernetes).

🎯

Drug Discovery Support

Target identification, drug-target-disease relationship modelling, knowledge graph design, and interrogation of large perturbation datasets (LINCS). Supporting pharma/biotech in early-stage discovery and target validation.

🧪

Sequence Analysis & Evolution

Comprehensive sequence analysis including BLAST, HMMER searches, multiple sequence alignment, phylogenetics, and molecular evolution studies. RNAseq analysis, differential expression, and gene set enrichment.

📈

Machine Learning & Statistical Modelling

Applying supervised and unsupervised ML methods (Random Forest, XGBoost, PCA, clustering) to biological data. Statistical analysis, longitudinal data modelling, and clinical data stratification for endotype identification.

🔬

Antibody Affinity Engineering

Structure-based antibody–antigen affinity maturation through stability-effect prediction: interface identification, CDR-aware PSSM scanning across all substitutions, and ΔΔG combinatorial multi-mutant design. Delivering ranked candidate sequences for experimental validation, parallelised for HPC.

⚙️

LLM-Assisted Automation

Designing LLM-assisted development patterns (Claude Code) to automate multistep bioinformatics analyses—accelerating pipeline development, reducing manual overhead, and establishing reusable building blocks for therapeutic-design workflows.
